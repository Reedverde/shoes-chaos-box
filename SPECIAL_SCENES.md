# Special scenes

## ARCCORE — Arc Core / Mr Genesis Special

- Trigger key: `ARCCORE`
- Card B index: `SPECIAL.CSV`
- Frame list: `SPECIAL/ARCCORE/FRAMES.CSV`
- Duration: 35,040 ms
- Device frames: 48 total — 16 startup frames at 315 ms, followed by 32
  powered-on pulse frames spread across 30 seconds
- Behavior: silent startup, four dramatic blue breathing pulses made from the
  real 3.10-to-3.68-second Kling source motion played backward and forward,
  then full repeat
- Normal randomized rotation: excluded
- Main control (pedal Mode 5): right pedal cycles QR → Arc Core → home.
  Arc Core loops silently between the second and third right-pedal presses.
- Alternate control (Mode 4): either pedal starts the loop and either pedal
  stops it on the next click
- Lighting: a fast accelerating gold chase makes seven revolutions, closes
  into a bright gold ring, transitions directly to blue by 5.04 seconds, then
  follows the 30-second powered-on section with four slow, high-contrast
  full-ring blue pulses

The special has no S-number and no DFPlayer track. This keeps it outside the
32 normal scene/halo codes and gives firmware a stable named target. The
startup retains its 315 ms holds. The 30-second powered-on section uses longer
holds for a calm breathing effect and stays within the 48-frame firmware limit.
